zPDF 0.1.7

Fixed

  • Signatures on rotated pages came out sideways. On a page shown turned (for example a landscape form stored as portrait), a digital signature or a Fill & Sign signature, initials or image was drawn as if the page weren't turned, so it appeared sideways and squeezed into the box. It now reads upright and fills the box you drew. To fix a signature already made, clear it in the Signatures panel and sign again.

Install or update

  • Already installed: choose zPDF ▸ Check for Updates….
  • New install: download zPDF-0.1.7.dmg, open it, and drag zPDF to Applications.

Requires macOS 15 or later on an Apple silicon Mac. For Windows, see the Windows release.
