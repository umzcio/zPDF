# zPDF 0.1.8

## New

- **Your own signature on digital signatures.** When you sign with a digital ID,
  choose what appears on the left of the signature box: **My signature**, **My
  name** or **Nothing**. Choose **Create…** to type your name in a script font,
  draw it with the trackpad or mouse, or import a photo or scan of your real
  signature (the paper background is removed). zPDF remembers your choice.
- **A true preview** of the signature box, in the shape of the box you drew.

## Install or update

- **Already installed:** choose **zPDF ▸ Check for Updates…**.
- **New install:** download `zPDF-0.1.8.dmg`, open it, and drag **zPDF** to
  **Applications**.

Requires macOS 15 or later on an Apple silicon Mac. For Windows, see the
Windows release.
