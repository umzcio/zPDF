zPDF 0.1.0 — first tester build

A native macOS PDF editor. This is an early build for testing: please report anything that looks wrong, breaks, or loses work.

Install

  • Download zPDF-0.1.0.dmg, open it, and drag zPDF to Applications.
  • Open zPDF from Applications. It's signed and notarized, so it opens normally.
  • Updates: zPDF ▸ Check for Updates… (it also checks automatically).

Requires macOS 15 or later on an Apple silicon Mac (M1 or newer).

What's in it

  • Edit PDF: edit text in place, add text and images, move/resize/crop images and artwork, links, crop pages, headers & footers, watermarks, backgrounds, Bates numbering, find & replace.
  • Redact: mark text, areas, pages or search patterns (SSN, phone, email, card numbers, dates); applying removes the underlying content; remove hidden information.
  • Comment: highlight, underline, strikethrough, replace/insert text, notes, text boxes, callouts, pen and eraser, shapes, stamps, attachments, sound, replies and status, import/export FDF/XFDF, summaries, flatten.
  • Forms & signatures: fill forms, Fill & Sign on any PDF, signatures and initials, Prepare Form (all field types, calculations, formats, tab order), password and certificate protection, digital signatures (PAdES) and validation, convert hybrid XFA forms to standard forms.
  • Pages & documents: insert, replace, duplicate, rotate, reorder, split, combine, page labels, page boxes; create PDFs from images, documents, web pages, the clipboard or a scanner; OCR; optimize and reduce file size; PDF/A, PDF/X and preflight; compare files.
  • Export: Word, Excel, PowerPoint, HTML, Markdown, RTF, XML, EPUB and images.
  • Reading: bookmarks, attachments, layers, destinations, advanced search, full screen, split view, measure tools, accessibility checker, read aloud, document properties, Action Wizard, custom print layouts.

Known limitations

  • Conversions to Office and other formats are close but not identical to the PDF; export notes list what changed.
  • Scanner and iPhone camera import, and microphone sound comments, haven't been tested on real hardware yet.
  • Dynamic XFA forms display their fallback pages only.
  • Apple silicon only; no Windows version yet.

Your files are only changed when you choose Save. Every edit can be undone.
